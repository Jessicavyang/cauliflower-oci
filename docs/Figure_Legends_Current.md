## Fig1 Image-derived feature illustrations

The original curd photographs, channel views and circular arrangement are retained. (a) Morphological measurements. (b) Radial structure illustration. (c) RGB and HSV channel illustrations. (d) Pseudocolour texture illustration. (e) Area and perimeter illustration. (f) Sectional dark-gap illustration. Panels b and d are illustrative visualizations, not additional measured descriptors or GLCM outputs. The restored OpenCV v8 implementation converts the image to grayscale, applies a 5 × 5 Gaussian filter and global Otsu thresholding, and fills the largest external curd contour. Gap count includes 8-connected dark components larger than five pixels; gap ratio includes all dark pixels within the filled curd mask. Twenty extracted variables comprise four morphology, five structure, six colour, three texture and two size descriptors. The implemented texture descriptors are grayscale-histogram entropy and angular second moment, and the standard deviation of the Laplacian response. Pixel-based size measurements have no verified conversion to millimetres. The central equipment image is an acquisition illustration and does not establish standardized illumination, colour calibration or physical-scale calibration.

## Fig2 Descriptive correlations between mechanical measurements and image-derived features

Pearson correlations were calculated from all 275 complete F₂ records, comprising two mechanical measurements and twenty image-derived features. Lower-triangle cells show r rounded to two decimals; upper-triangle sectors encode its magnitude and sign. Red indicates positive and blue negative correlation. The outlined block identifies GR and DD. GR is the exported force–time gradient (gf s⁻¹), not peak force or force–displacement stiffness. DD is deformation distance (mm), referenced to the 5 gf trigger and recorded at the 30,000 gf target load according to the author-confirmed measurement definition. The precise GR marker interval and DD software readout remain unresolved. These full-cohort correlations describe associations and were not the training candidate-screening results. Model screening used the relevant training data. No significance testing or causal interpretation is encoded in this figure.

These correlation loadings are distinct from orthonormal eigenvector coefficients, which are supplied separately in Source Data. (c) Training scores (circles, n = 220) and historical test projections (triangles, n = 55) on the training-fitted axes. Colour denotes the frozen OCI score, not a PCA-derived comprehensive score. ## Fig5 Research-defined grading of frozen OCI scores

The stacked histogram shows all 275 F₂ records in 35 equal-width bins on the training-fitted OCI scale. The frozen boundaries, 21.6883357476 and 49.6318049088, define Compact (n = 137), Medium (n = 101) and Loose (n = 37), respectively. They were obtained from k = 3 clustering of TRAIN220 scores and then applied without refitting. The three groups are study-defined categories, not validated official DUS grades. OCI is a mechanically calibrated phenotype derived from a two-dimensional longitudinal-section image; it is not three-dimensional porosity or an absolute compactness truth. Scores 0 and 100 are training minimum and maximum anchors. The scoring rule does not clip observations to that range.

## SupplementFig3 Descriptive sensitivity to the number of OCI clusters

The original dual-axis, three-curve design is retained for k = 2–6 using the same frozen TRAIN220 OCI scores (n = 220). The grey curve shows archived training silhouette scores (left axis). Blue and red curves show the descriptive fractions of GR and DD variance explained by the corresponding OCI clusters (η², right axis). Cluster assignments were replayed using the archived ascending centres and checked against the archived within-cluster sums of squares; no clustering or model-selection procedure was rerun. For each mechanical variable, η² is the between-cluster sum of squares divided by its total training sum of squares. These post hoc quantities were not used to select k or tune the frozen boundaries. The dashed line identifies the study’s k = 3 specification. This descriptive comparison does not establish a biological optimum, independent structural validity or maximal silhouette score at k = 3.
